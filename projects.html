01

Sea Water Purifier Monitoring

Problem
Read industrial equipment measurements and make them usable in a monitoring dashboard.
Tools
Modbus ASCII, MOXA gateway, Python, Flask, MongoDB
My work
During my Tech Mahindra internship, I decoded Modbus ASCII frames, checked LRC values, and mapped registers from equipment connected through a MOXA gateway. I also helped present the measurements in a responsive dashboard.
Result & notes
The dashboard supported real-time monitoring. My internship summary reports decoding more than 10,000 sensor packets daily. This work taught me to check the data before relying on what the interface displays.

05

Traffic Signal Control

Problem
Compare learned traffic-signal policies with a fixed-time policy in a simulated intersection.
Tools
Python, PyTorch; DQN, Double DQN, Dueling DQN, Q-Learning, SARSA, fixed-time baseline
My work
In this two-author project, we built a custom intersection simulator and a shared evaluation harness for six policies. Initial learned policies underperformed. We revised the reward around queue length, added a minimum-green constraint, and included phase duration in the state.
Result & notes
After the redesign, deep-RL agents reduced average waiting time by roughly 78% against the fixed-time baseline over 20 evaluation episodes while maintaining throughput. This was a simulator result, not a measured improvement on real roads.
